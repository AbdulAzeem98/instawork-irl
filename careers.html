Role 01 — Data Captain / Field Sales

Data Captain

The Commercial Data Captain is responsible for onboarding commercial businesses onto the Instawork data collection platform, managing a team of Field Officers, and ensuring maximum recording hours across all active locations. The role combines business development, team management, and operational execution.

Earn up to
₹1,01,000
per week (incentives)
Key Responsibilities
  • Identify, pitch, and onboard commercial businesses — retail shops, kitchens, construction sites, workshops — for egocentric video data collection
  • Ensure weekly hours targets are met across all onboarded businesses to hit slab bonuses
  • Manage Field Officers stationed at business locations to supervise recording
  • Coordinate device allocation with the office — ensure the right number of devices reach each location
  • Maintain the business pipeline in the tracking sheet
  • Ensure business owners understand the payout structure and are motivated to maximise recording hours per device
Business Development & Onboarding
  • Visit 8–10 commercial businesses daily to pitch the data collection opportunity
  • Deliver the standard pitch clearly and confidently to business owners
  • Handle common objections — what it's for, how long, is it legal, rate negotiation
  • Log every business in the tracking sheet immediately after onboarding
  • Call Field Officers at slow locations — the #1 failure mode is collectors taking breaks
  • Ensure FOs are pushing for 6+ hours per device per day at every location
Daily Rhythm
  • Morning: Field visits to new businesses — pitch, log in sheet
  • Afternoon: Check recording status with FOs, call slow locations, update pipeline sheet, plan next day
  • End of day: Review total hours across all locations, calculate progress toward weekly slab

Role 02 — Field Officer

Field Officer

The Field Officer is stationed at onboarded business locations to supervise and support the day-to-day recording process. You are the boots on the ground — ensuring devices are running, workers are comfortable, and recording hours are maximised at every location you manage.

Base Pay
₹30,000
+ ₹6,000 petrol incentive/month
Key Responsibilities
  • Be stationed at assigned business locations during recording hours
  • Ensure workers are wearing devices correctly and recording is active at all times
  • Monitor device-hours logged daily and push for 6+ hours per device per day
  • Coordinate with the Data Captain on device logistics — how many devices are needed, when a location finishes, when to move to the next business
  • Escalate any issues — worker breaks, device problems, business owner concerns — to the Data Captain promptly
  • Update recording status and device utilisation in real time
Daily Rhythm
  • Morning: Arrive at assigned business location, set up devices, brief workers
  • During the day: Monitor recording, keep workers motivated, ensure no unnecessary breaks
  • Afternoon: Update Data Captain on hours logged, flag slow locations immediately
  • End of day: Collect devices, confirm hours, report to Data Captain for next day planning
Compensation
💼 Base Salary: ₹30,000/month (fixed) + ₹6,000/month petrol incentive = ₹36,000/month guaranteed

Skills & Eligibility

Strong communication skills — you work directly with business owners and their workers daily
Ability to keep workers motivated and on track throughout the day
Comfortable spending full days at business locations
Basic smartphone and app proficiency
Good knowledge of Bengaluru areas
Self-driven, punctual, and reliable
Own smartphone required; two-wheeler preferred

Key Performance Indicators

Daily device-hours logged at your assigned locations
Average hours per device per day (target: 6+ hours)
Worker participation rate — all assigned workers recording
Device utilisation — no idle devices during working hours
Issue escalation speed — problems reported to Data Captain immediately
Attendance and punctuality at assigned locations
