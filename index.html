Common questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is being recorded?
Only hand and wrist movements. The device is positioned to capture how hands interact with objects, tools, and surfaces during normal work. No faces, no voices, no background footage.
Is this legal? Do we need any permissions?
Yes, completely legal. We sign a formal agreement with the business owner before any recording begins. You have full control and can stop at any time. The agreement protects both parties.
Will work get disrupted?
Not at all. Workers wear a small device on their wrist and continue doing exactly what they always do. Our Field Officer is present to manage everything. Most business owners say they forget the recording is even happening.
How and when do we get paid?
Payment is calculated based on total device-hours recorded per week and transferred directly to your bank account within 2–3 business days. The more hours recorded, the higher your payout slab.
How long does the recording last?
Typically 2–3 days per location. We bring our devices, set up, record for the agreed days, then collect the devices and leave. It's a short, clean engagement with no long-term commitment.
Who is Instawork and can we trust them?
Instawork is a US-based company backed by top investors including Benchmark, Y Combinator, Greylock, and Spark Capital. The IRL program is their initiative to collect real-world movement data for robotics research. They are a legitimate, funded company operating globally.
About us

About Instawork & IRL

Instawork is on a mission to create meaningful economic opportunities for skilled hourly professionals around the globe. Their AI-powered platform connects local businesses with workers at scale.

IRL — which stands for In Real Life — is Instawork's answer to a massive challenge in AI: the "100,000-year data gap" identified by UC Berkeley researchers. Robots need to learn from real human work, and that's exactly what this program collects.

By deploying skilled workers into real commercial environments — kitchens, warehouses, workshops — IRL captures the high-fidelity data that the world's leading robotics labs use to train foundation models.
