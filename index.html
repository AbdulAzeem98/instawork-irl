Common questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is being recorded?
Only hand and wrist movements. The device is positioned to capture how hands interact with objects, tools, and surfaces during normal work. No faces, no voices, no background footage of any kind.
Is this legal?
Yes, 100% legal. Fully compliant with all applicable laws. You have full control at every stage of the process.
Will work get disrupted?
Not at all. Workers wear a small device and continue doing exactly what they always do. Our Field Officer is present to manage everything on the ground. Most business owners say they forget the recording is even happening.
How and when do we get paid?
Payment is calculated based on total device-hours recorded per week and transferred directly to your bank account. The more hours recorded, the higher your payout slab.
Who is Instawork and can we trust them?
Instawork is a US-based company backed by top investors including Benchmark, Y Combinator, Greylock, and Spark Capital. The IRL program is their initiative to collect real-world movement data for robotics research. They are a legitimate, well-funded company operating globally.
About us

About Instawork & IRL

Instawork is on a mission to create meaningful economic opportunities for skilled hourly professionals around the globe. Their AI-powered platform connects local businesses with workers at scale.

IRL — In Real Life — is Instawork's answer to a massive challenge in AI: the "100,000-year data gap" identified by UC Berkeley researchers. Robots need to learn from real human work, and that's exactly what this program collects.

By deploying workers into real commercial environments — kitchens, warehouses, workshops — IRL captures high-fidelity data that the world's leading robotics labs use to train foundation models.
